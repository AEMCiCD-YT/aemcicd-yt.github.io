About AEMCiCD

AEMCiCD represents and supports students in Mathematics, Computer Science, and Data Science at Yachay Tech University. The association promotes academic, scientific, cultural, social, and student-representation activities.

AEMCiCD was granted legal personality by the Ministry of Education, Sports and Culture of Ecuador and is registered in the Unified System of Social Organizations (SUIOS). The association is organized as an autonomous nonprofit student organization with educational and social purposes.

Mission

Our mission is to strengthen student participation, academic development, community building, institutional transparency, and democratic governance within the student community of Mathematics, Computer Science, and Data Science.

Student representation

We represent student interests before internal and external authorities and promote dialogue around academic and institutional needs.

Academic development

We promote talks, workshops, study groups, conferences, and scientific activities related to mathematics and computing.

Digital governance

We develop and maintain tools that support transparent elections, assemblies, records, documents, and institutional participation.

Technology projects

AEMCiCD uses technology to support nonprofit, educational, and institutional activities. Our GitHub organization is used to host and maintain projects that strengthen student governance, transparency, and participation.

Main project

Votia — Digital governance and elections platform

Votia is a digital platform developed to support student organizations with secure elections, assemblies, attendance registration, voting, minutes, resolutions, document archives, and transparency workflows.

  • Digital election process management.
  • Attendance and quorum registration for assemblies.
  • Voting workflows for governance sessions.
  • Minutes, resolutions, and document archive support.
  • Transparency and institutional continuity for student organizations.

Use of GitHub

We use GitHub to organize software development, documentation, issue tracking, code review, and continuity of nonprofit technology projects. GitHub helps us preserve institutional ownership of projects even when student leaders graduate or change roles.

  • Repository hosting for nonprofit technology projects.
  • Issue tracking and technical roadmap management.
  • Code review, branch protection, and secure collaboration.
  • Continuous integration and deployment workflows.
  • Technical documentation and long-term project continuity.
