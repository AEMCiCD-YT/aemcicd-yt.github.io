Participation and institutional memory

Recognizing the work that sustains the community.

The association is also built through the time, initiative and effort of those who contribute to its activities, projects and support teams.

Recognition criteria

How we document contributions

Institutional memory is built on identifiable contributions and reviewed evidence.

To propose recognition, identify the activity or project, the period and the specific contribution. The team reviews the information and agrees with the person involved on what can be published. Names, photos and descriptions are not published without that review and permission.

A proposal should describe the contribution: for example, coordinating an activity, technical work, tutoring or logistics support. A nomination is not an award already granted. You can consult the people who have held board responsibilities and their institutional history.
