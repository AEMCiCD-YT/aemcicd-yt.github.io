Our mission

Supporting students' learning and participation

The Student Association for Mathematics, Computer Science and Data Science at Yachay Tech represents its community in Urcuquí, Imbabura. We work to make room for dialogue about students' needs, turn their ideas into initiatives and complement their education with collaborative experiences inside and outside the classroom.

We put this mission into practice through academic activities, peer support, meetings with companies and technology projects with a social purpose. Conecta YT aims to connect university talent with the professional world; BrailleLab Ecuador offers hands-on engineering learning through open, repairable Braille technology.

The association serves social and academic purposes. Project targets are presented as goals, and results are shared when they can be verified.
