Conecta YT 2026: from the classroom to your first professional opportunity.

Conecta YT is the event through which AEMCiCD and the IEEE Yachay Tech Student Branch connect students and young professionals with companies, organizations and university communities across the country. It takes place on Saturday, November 21, 2026 in Ibarra, Imbabura.

Attend Conecta Empresas (free) Explore both gatherings
Why we organize it

Responding to a student need

Conecta YT grows from the association's mission: to represent, support and connect its community.

The challenge

Moving from the classroom into a professional setting means learning what companies do, how they accept applications and what skills they need. Conecta aims to give students a place to ask these questions, present their interests and explore learning and professional experience opportunities.

Our approach

We bring people seeking opportunities and those offering them together in the same place on the same day. Free entry to the fair helps prevent cost from becoming a barrier, while the inter-university gathering strengthens connections among student branches, clubs and young professionals.

Our aim
For each participant to leave with real contacts, information about internships and employment, and a clearer idea of how to present their work.
Who it is for
Students and young professionals from any university, in fields such as computer science, data science, mathematics, engineering, natural sciences, life sciences, business and design.
Its connection to AEMCiCD
It is a student outreach and support activity within the association's purposes. AEMCiCD is responsible for its documentation, contracts and financial management.

What you can do at Conecta

Conversations to help you plan your next step

Explore opportunities with companies

Ask about the profiles each organization seeks, its requirements and application channels. You can bring your CV and a brief introduction to your projects. Each company decides whether to accept documents during the gathering or refer you to its regular process.

Discover technology and learning opportunities

Demonstrations show the problems that the featured products and services address. Confirmed talks and training opportunities provide another way to connect university learning with professional applications and skills.

Expand your university network

Conecta Universidades focuses on sharing experiences among student branches, clubs and young professionals. Its purpose is to encourage connections and collaboration between communities at different institutions.

Plan your participation

Review the agenda and confirmed participants before attending. Decide which organizations you want to meet and prepare specific questions. Attendance or preregistration does not guarantee employment, placements, internships, scholarships or admission to courses: each organization sets its own offering and requirements.

Conecta is an AEMCiCD outreach activity: it complements academic education through professional connections and student collaboration. Its organization and use of resources follow the association's social and academic purposes.

Transparency

How it is funded

The event is supported by contributions from participating companies and Conecta Universidades registration fees.

  • Companies contribute for their stand or participation through a written agreement with AEMCiCD and receive the corresponding tax receipt.
  • Funds are received only in the association's institutional account, never in personal accounts.
  • Resources support event organization: venues, logistics, materials and operations. Every receipt and expense is recorded for the final financial settlement.
  • Any surpluses support the purposes of the participating student organizations under the co-organization agreements and financial reporting process. They do not generate personal profit.
  • We do not sell or transfer attendees' data.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to register to attend Conecta Empresas?

No. Entry is free and you can arrive without registering. Preregistration is optional: it helps us estimate attendance and gives you a QR code for faster entry.

Can I attend both gatherings?

Yes. When registering for Conecta Universidades, you can indicate that you will also attend Conecta Empresas without completing another form.

Do I need to be a Yachay Tech student or IEEE member?

No. Both gatherings are open to students and young professionals from any university. IEEE membership only changes the registration category for Conecta Universidades.

What should I bring to the fair?

An updated CV if you are looking for internships or employment, and a brief way to present your projects and skills. Each company determines how it accepts applications.

Do all companies offer jobs or internships?

Not necessarily. Some present career or learning opportunities; others showcase products, services or technology. Check each participant's offering and application channel. The event facilitates contact and does not take part in candidate selection.

How can a company or organization participate?

Write to empresas@conectayt.com. Participation is formalized through a written agreement with AEMCiCD; an inquiry does not reserve space or benefits.

How can a student branch or club participate?

Write to universidades@conectayt.com to coordinate your community's participation in Conecta Universidades.
