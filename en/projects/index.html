Why we support projects

Learning, participating and collaborating beyond the classroom

AEMCiCD projects address different needs in our community. Conecta YT aims to connect students with companies and other universities; BrailleLab Ecuador applies technology skills to accessibility; academic and community initiatives complement learning and student support.

Each initiative's description distinguishes its purpose, audience and status. Prototypes and future calls are presented as work in progress. Planned targets are not achieved results.

BrailleLab Ecuador

An ongoing AEMCiCD program for learning, designing, building and documenting electronic refreshable Braille technology with open hardware and user involvement in validation.

Its 2027 technical campaign gathers support for kits and prototypes, with targets, expenses and progress published on its transparency page.

Program · Registration open

AEMCiCD AI Program

Student access to institutional Claude and ChatGPT Business seats for Yachay Tech students. Participant payments cover the service, taxes and management expenses under approved fees; they pay for access and are not donations. Any operating surpluses remain with the association for its institutional purposes.

Check the program form for current plans, prices and conditions before confirming participation. Monthly payments are due from the 1st to the 4th of each month. Passwords and account content are not requested.

Program · Registration open

AEMCiCD LaTeX Program

A collaborative, browser-based LaTeX editing platform hosted and managed by AEMCiCD for Yachay Tech students. It runs on Overleaf Community Edition with open-source extensions, so students can write theses, reports and conference or journal papers without installing LaTeX.

It is a service of the association, independent of Overleaf and its paid plans. Participant payments cover the server, operations and administrative costs under the approved rates; they are payments for access, not donations. Check the plans, current prices and conditions on the program form before confirming your participation.

Commercial activity

Votia

A technology platform developed and managed by AEMCiCD for elections, governance, assemblies, meetings, attendance, voting, minutes, resolutions and document archives.

Its contracts are an economic activity of the association: revenue covers development, infrastructure and operations. Surpluses are reinvested in academic and social purposes, institutional development or reserves, without distribution to members. Contracts are recorded separately from donations and sponsorships.

Understanding each initiative's status

Labels explain the current scope and distinguish projects, activities and services.

Official · Production

An initiative recognized by the association and currently available in the stated condition.

Program · Ongoing

An ongoing area of work, with editions or initiatives that may vary each year.

Registration open

An event with a confirmed date and an official registration or preregistration channel available.

Commercial activity

A product or service managed commercially and separately from donations and contributions to the association.

Approval pending

An initiative that exists as a proposal or project but still needs permissions or other prerequisites.

Academic

An area of work related to learning, outreach, tutoring or support.
