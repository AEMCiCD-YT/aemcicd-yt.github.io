Purpose of resources

Supporting social and academic purposes

AEMCiCD is a nonprofit organization. The resources it manages must serve its purposes: representation, academic and social development, student activities and projects consistent with its mission. Articles 3, 8 and 10 of the public bylaws provide the basis for this work.

BrailleLab Ecuador

The technical campaign supports kits, materials and prototyping. Its target is a planned budget; financial progress distinguishes funds received, spent and committed as of a stated date. In-kind contributions are identified separately from cash income.

Conecta YT

View organization and funding details →

Votia revenue covers service development and operations. Surpluses remain with AEMCiCD and are reinvested in academic and social purposes, institutional development or reserves. No dividends are distributed to members. Articles 65 and 66 of the bylaws establish the institutional purpose of funds and the absence of a right to dividends from contributions. Contracts are recorded separately from donations and sponsorships. AEMCiCD AI Program payments cover contracted access and related expenses under approved fees. Operating surpluses belong to the association and are not donations. Questions about the use of a contribution or public documentation can be sent to asoemc@yachaytech.edu.ec.

Publication criteria

Transparency also means publishing responsibly.

What we publish

Association information

Verifiable legal information, reviewed documents, boards, projects and official contact channels.

What we protect

Personal data

ID numbers, signatures, private contacts, personal or donor data and bank receipts, and documents not reviewed for publication. The only public banking instructions are those for the receiving institutional account when authorized for a campaign.

Approval

Editorial responsibility

The President reviews and approves significant changes before public content is updated.

Updates

Editorial responsibility rests with the President.

Association information is reviewed before significant changes to the board, documents, projects or support channels are published.
