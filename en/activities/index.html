Areas of work

Activities for learning, participating and connecting.

The association's activities focus on academic life, community and student representation.

Academic

Talks and learning

We promote seminars, courses, competitions, tutoring, study groups and skill development in mathematics, computer science and data science.

Community

Community building and support

We encourage community activities, peer support and connections between students across degree programs and year groups.

Representation

Institutional dialogue

We bring students' needs, proposals and concerns to internal or external bodies in line with the association's purposes.

Events

Gatherings and conferences

We organize or support scientific, academic and cultural activities connecting students, faculty, organizations and institutions.

Transparency

Records and documentation

We encourage organized minutes, resolutions, reports and documents to strengthen accountability and institutional continuity.

Technology

Institutional tools

We develop digital solutions for elections, governance, documentation and organized participation.

Activities with a purpose

How we complement the university experience

Tutoring and study groups allow students to work through specific questions together. Talks introduce the community to new fields. Meetings with organizations provide an opportunity to ask how academic skills are used in professional settings. The association connects these forms of participation with its purposes and the people responsible for each initiative.

In Conecta YT, the focus is on connections between students, companies and university communities. In BrailleLab Ecuador, learning centers on designing and documenting Braille technology prototypes. Each project's call explains its audience and how to take part.

To propose an activity, write to the association's official contact channel with the topic, intended audience and support you can offer. Coordination must establish responsibilities and conditions before a call is announced.
