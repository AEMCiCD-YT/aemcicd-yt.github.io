Privacy notice

How we handle data on this website.

This notice explains what data is processed when you visit aemcicd.org, for what purpose and how to exercise your rights under Ecuador's Personal Data Protection Law. Effective October 10, 2026.

Responsible organization

Asociación de Estudiantes de Matemática, Ciencias Computacionales y Ciencia de Datos de la Universidad Yachay Tech (AEMCiCD), tax ID (RUC) 1091799923001, Vía Yachay s/n, Urcuquí, Imbabura, Ecuador. Privacy contact: asoemc@yachaytech.edu.ec.

What data is processed

This website has no forms or user accounts and does not ask for your name, email or bank details.

Advertising measurement

Google Ads tag

We use the Google Ads tag to understand whether our ads, funded by Google Ad Grants, lead to useful actions: clicks to the BrailleLab campaign and call for participation, and clicks on the association's email. Google may use cookies and browser identifiers, your IP address, the page visited and the ad you came from. Google processes this data under its own policy.

Hosting

Technical logs

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like other web servers, it may record technical connection data, such as IP addresses, to operate and protect the service.

Email

If you write to us

If you email us, we use your data only to respond and coordinate your request.

Other websites

BrailleLab contributions and registration are managed at braillelab.org, which has its own privacy notice. Social networks and other linked websites apply their own policies.

BrailleLab privacy notice

How to limit measurement

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, use a content blocker or manage personalized advertising in your Google account. The website will continue to work normally.

Your rights

You may request access, correction and updates, deletion, objection, portability and suspension of the processing of your data by writing to asoemc@yachaytech.edu.ec. For data processed by Google, you can also exercise your rights directly with Google. If you believe your rights have not been addressed, you may contact Ecuador's Superintendence of Personal Data Protection.
