About AEMCiCD

We are the Student Association for Mathematics, Computer Science and Data Science at Yachay Tech University, an autonomous Ecuadorian nonprofit organization under private law, serving social and academic purposes.

Our community is connected to Yachay Tech's Mathematics, Computer Science and Data Science degree programs. From Urcuquí, Imbabura, we bring together student representation, learning, community activities and projects in collaboration with other communities and organizations.

AEMCiCD's tax ID (RUC) is 1091799923001 and its SUIOS registration number is 0000146391. Its address is Vía Yachay s/n, University Campus, Urcuquí, Imbabura, Ecuador.

Institutional independence

AEMCiCD acts as an independent student organization. The reference to Yachay Tech University in our name identifies the community we represent.

The name does not imply official representation, sponsorship or institutional endorsement by the university.
Our purpose

Our mission

To defend our members' rights and contribute to their academic and social development through representation, dialogue, learning activities and opportunities to collaborate. We want students to take part in decisions affecting their community and develop skills for university and professional life.

To do this, we gather students' needs, encourage academic exchange, support scientific and community activities and organize projects where people learn by working together. This description summarizes the purposes and objectives in Articles 3, 8 and 10 of our association bylaws.

Who we represent

The association represents Yachay Tech students in Mathematics, Computer Science and Data Science. Participation by members, graduates and collaborators follows the categories and conditions in the bylaws.

Who our projects reach

Some initiatives also invite students from other universities, young professionals, faculty and organizations. Each project explains its audience and participation process: collaborating or attending an activity does not change a person's membership status.
