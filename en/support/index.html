What your support makes possible

Resources for learning and project development

Collaboration with the association has a specific purpose: enabling activities and projects with academic or community benefits. BrailleLab Ecuador's technical contributions support components, materials and prototyping; Conecta YT participation agreements support event organization and logistics.

Before contributing, check the initiative's scope and conditions. The team must identify how support will be used and the applicable agreement. Funding targets describe what the campaign aims to raise; confirmed income and expenses are reported through each campaign's transparency channels.

Votia has a separate channel

A commercial relationship is not a donation to the association.

Commercial activity

Votia is a technology platform developed and managed by AEMCiCD for elections and institutional governance. Contract revenue covers Votia's development and operations. Surpluses are reinvested in the association's academic and social purposes, institutional development or reserves; they are not distributed to members. Contracts are recorded separately from donations and sponsorships.

How to start a collaboration

Initial contact is currently handled through the association's email.

Indicate the type of support, activity or project you are interested in.
2. We coordinate
We review the purpose, scope, responsibilities and conditions.
3. We approve
Sponsor benefits, logos or conditions require a written agreement.
Collaborations are coordinated through the association's email and formalized under each agreement. Instructions for the receiving institutional account are published only when explicitly authorized for a campaign; never personal accounts or contributors' receipts or bank details. A donation does not grant visibility benefits: logos or mentions require a written agreement, and sales or return on investment are not guaranteed.
